*Goals and Objectives:*
1. To ease the task of accesing the emails on WhatsApp. 
2. To ease the process of integration of email accessing through multi-thread. 
3. To fetch the meta data of the emails efficiently. 
4. To send the email metadata to the user.

*Problem Definition:*
To ease the process of email handling through interactive multi-thread to read the emails 
from the Gmail host and send the notifications on WhatsApp with meta metadata of the emails 
using decision making technic.
